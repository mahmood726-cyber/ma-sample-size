How to Use

1. Select outcome type (binary or continuous).
2. Enter the expected event rates or effect size.
3. Set your alpha level, target power, and expected heterogeneity.
4. Enter the average size of studies you expect to find.
5. Click Calculate Required Information Size.

Use Power Curves to explore how results change with different parameters.

Use Existing Evidence if you already have a meta-analysis and want to assess its power.

Key Concepts

Required Information Size (RIS): Minimum total participants needed across all studies for the meta-analysis to be conclusive — analogous to a single adequately-powered trial, adjusted upward for between-study heterogeneity.

D² adjustment: Heterogeneity inflation factor = I²/(1−I²). At I²=50%, RIS doubles. At I²=75%, RIS quadruples.

Studies needed (k): RIS divided by expected mean study size × 2 (both arms).

About This Tool

This panel calculates the post-hoc power of an existing or planned meta-analysis.

Enter your current k, N, observed effect, and I². The tool will:
• Compute current power vs the Required Information Size
• Show additional studies and participants needed
• Predict marginal power gain from one additional study
• Classify evidence as conclusive (Green), borderline (Yellow), or underpowered (Red)
